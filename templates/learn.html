Cybersecurity Curriculum

Master the concepts before you enter the Arena.

Phishing

Phishing is a cybercrime in which a target or targets are contacted by email, telephone or text message by someone posing as a legitimate institution to lure individuals into providing sensitive data.

Warning Signs
  • Urgent language (e.g., "Account suspended", "Action required now")
  • Mismatched or spoofed sender addresses
  • Suspicious links (hover before clicking)
  • Requests for credentials or payment

Password Security

Strong passwords are your first line of defense against cyber attacks. A weak password can be cracked in seconds.

Best Practices
  • Use passphrases (multiple words joined together)
  • Never reuse passwords across different accounts
  • Use a reputable password manager
  • Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
Test Your Password Skills

Social Engineering

The psychological manipulation of people into performing actions or divulging confidential information. It relies on human error rather than technical vulnerabilities.

Common Tactics
  • Pretexting: Creating a fabricated scenario (e.g., fake IT support)
  • Baiting: Offering something enticing to trick the victim
  • Quid Pro Quo: Requesting info in exchange for a service
Test Your SE Skills

Digital Payment Safety

As digital payments (UPI, wallets, cards) become common, scammers invent new ways to steal money directly from your accounts.

Prevention Tips
  • You DO NOT need to enter your PIN to receive money
  • Never scan unknown QR codes to get a refund or prize
  • Verify the recipient's name before sending funds
